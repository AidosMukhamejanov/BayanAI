PRIMARY LEGAL BASIS
- Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 on cosmetic products, consolidated text 18 May 2026.
- Annex II: prohibited substances.
- Annex III: restricted substances.
- Annex IV: allowed colorants subject to conditions.
- Annex V: allowed preservatives subject to conditions.
- Annex VI: allowed UV filters subject to conditions.

IMPORTANT
PERMITTED_BASELINE_MVP is an engineering whitelist for the BayanAI MVP.
It means that the ingredient is a common cosmetic INCI ingredient and this
dataset does not identify a specific Annex II/III prohibition/restriction for
ordinary cosmetic use. It is NOT a legal statement that every formulation
containing the ingredient is automatically compliant or medically safe.

============================================================
DECISION RULES FOR BAYANAI
============================================================

1. Exact match in BANNED -> banned.
2. Exact match in RESTRICTED -> restricted.
3. Exact match in ALLOWED_* -> safe only if supplied conditions can be verified; otherwise restricted/needs_review.
4. Exact match in PERMITTED_BASELINE_MVP -> safe for MVP screening, with note that this is not full legal approval.
5. No match -> unknown.
6. Never interpret unknown as safe.
7. Never claim medical safety.
