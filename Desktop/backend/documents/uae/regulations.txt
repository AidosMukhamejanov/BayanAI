BAYANAI REGULATORY DATASET
REGION: UNITED ARAB EMIRATES
VERSION: MVP dataset, prepared 2026-09-14

PRIMARY LEGAL BASIS
- GSO 1943:2024, Cosmetic Products – Safety Requirements of Cosmetics and Personal Care Products.
- GSO 1943:2024 references Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and its current amendments for the prohibited/restricted substances and the positive lists of colorants, preservatives and UV filters.
- The Saudi-specific deviation stated in GSO 1943:2024 does not apply to the UAE dataset.

IMPORTANT
PERMITTED_BASELINE_MVP is an engineering whitelist for the BayanAI MVP.
It is NOT an official UAE positive list for all cosmetic ingredients.
It means that this MVP dataset does not identify a specific prohibition or
restriction for ordinary cosmetic use. Full UAE compliance can still depend
on formulation, concentration, product type, claims, testing and registration.

============================================================
ADDITIONAL UAE/GSO REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

- Compliance is not determined by ingredients alone.
- GSO 1943:2024 also covers product safety, labelling, packaging and claims.
- Microbiological limits and relevant testing requirements apply.
- GMP requirements apply.
- Sunscreen products have additional GSO requirements.
- Product claims must comply with GSO claims rules.
- Ingredient concentration must not be invented if not supplied.

============================================================
DECISION RULES FOR BAYANAI
============================================================

1. Exact match in BANNED -> banned.
2. Exact match in RESTRICTED -> restricted.
3. Exact match in ALLOWED_* -> safe only if all supplied conditions are satisfied; otherwise restricted/needs_review.
4. Exact match in PERMITTED_BASELINE_MVP -> safe for MVP screening, with note that this is not full UAE product approval.
5. No match -> unknown.
6. Never interpret unknown as safe.
7. Never claim medical safety or full UAE registration compliance from ingredient screening alone.
